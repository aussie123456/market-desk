        Definitions and sources

        Market cap is the last price times basic shares outstanding from Yahoo Finance when live, otherwise from the latest filing.

        Enterprise value is market cap plus total debt, with convertible notes at face value, less cash and equivalents. Bitcoin holdings are excluded from cash.

        EV / adjusted EBITDA uses the sum of the last four reported quarters of IREN adjusted EBITDA. IREN's fiscal year ends 30 June; quarters are labelled by calendar quarter end.

        EV / MW divides EV by operating (energised) MW as reported at the latest quarter end. Contracted capacity is shown separately.

        Historical multiples rebuild EV at each month-end from that month's closing price and the most recent reported share count, debt and cash at that date, so each point is comparable.

        Broker consensus comes from public aggregators. Their analyst counts differ because each uses a different look-back window.

        Capital raises are gross proceeds at pricing, including exercised greenshoes where closed. Strategic equity placements are included as equity. Project-level bonds for sites tenanted by a peer appear under the sector tape, not under the peer.

        Pipeline status labels: filed (shelf or ATM on file), announced (company-stated plan), reported (press report, not company-confirmed).

        Look-through debt lists every borrowing by the entity that owes it. HoldCo debt is recourse to IREN Limited. SPV debt sits in a subsidiary: non-recourse where the parent gives no payment guarantee, guaranteed where it does. Reported debt is the consolidated balance-sheet figure, which already includes drawn SPV debt. Committed undrawn amounts are facility headroom not yet borrowed; fully drawn look-through adds them to reported debt. Facilities whose terms are not public are listed without a figure.

        Project timeline shows only milestones IREN has stated publicly. Filled markers are completed events on their announced date; hollow markers are targets, placed at the end of the stated quarter or year. Bars run from the first public milestone to the latest target. Sites in the pipeline without a stated date appear in the table only.

        Refresh is manual. The Refresh data button opens the refresh job; pressing Run there re-pulls market data, FRED series, news feeds and SEC filings, scans for new capital raises, and republishes this page within a few minutes. Cap stack, raises, pipeline and capacity history are curated from filings and updated by that job when it finds a new announcement.

        Informational only. Public sources: Yahoo Finance, FRED, SEC EDGAR, Google News, company filings and press releases. Figures are as reported by those sources and may lag.
